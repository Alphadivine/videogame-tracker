How to use

Everything the tracker can do — here whenever you need it.
➕

Add a game

Tap ＋ Add a game and start typing a title. Pick a suggestion and it pulls the cover art, release date, platforms and official link from the web automatically. Add a price if you like, then Add game. You can also paste an image URL for the cover.

✎

Edit or remove

Hover a card (or tap on mobile) and use the ✎ button to change the date, price, platforms, links or cover — handy when a release slips. Built-in games have a ↺ Reset to restore the original details. Games you added show a ✕ to remove them.

★

Priority rating

Tap the stars on a card to mark how excited you are, then use Sort → Most anticipated to float your top picks to the top.

✅

Track your status

Mark each game Want, Pre-ordered or Got it. The My list filter shows only games you've flagged.

💰

Budget

Games marked Want or Pre-ordered are totalled at the top and broken down by month, so you can see upcoming spend at a glance. (Free-to-play counts as $0.)

🛒

Buy where you want

Tap Buy ▾ to choose your platform — it opens that game on the right store: PlayStation Store, Microsoft/Xbox, Steam or Epic for PC, or the Nintendo eShop.

▶️

Trailer & calendar

▶ Trailer opens the game's trailer. ＋ Google adds the release date to your Google Calendar so you get a reminder.

🔎

Search, sort & group

Use the search box to find a game, Sort by soonest, price, or most anticipated, and toggle Group by month to organise the list by release month.

⏱️

Countdowns & the backdrop

Every card counts down live. The page background shows the next release's art and follows whichever card you hover. When a countdown hits zero you get a little confetti and the game tucks into the Released section at the bottom.

☁️

Sync across devices

Open ☁ Sync. Tap Turn on sync. You'll get a sync code — enter that on your other devices and your list stays in sync automatically. There's also a manual backup (download a file or copy a code) in the same panel that needs no account.

📲

Install it as an app

When it's hosted at a web link, add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share ▸ Add to Home Screen; Android/Desktop Chrome: Install app) for a full-screen app with its own icon that works offline.

Get this game

Choose where to buy — opens that store's page for this game.
